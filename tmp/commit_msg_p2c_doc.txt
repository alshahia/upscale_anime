Phase 2.C.doc: cascade 2x+2x hypothesis didn't pan out

The Phase 2 plan's premise -- that a 2x student applied twice would
be ~1.5-2x faster than a single 4x student -- turned out to be wrong
for RFDN's body-heavy architecture. Both quality and FPS regressed.

Updated docs/plans/todos_phase2_cascade_2026_08.md with measurements
and a list of follow-ups worth investigating (CUDA graphs, NVDEC
decode that actually engages, smaller student). Keeps the ship
banner honest for anyone reading the branch later.
